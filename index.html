How it works

Your kitchen, your menu

Set up once in under a minute. After that, the app is just your family's own menu card.

Step 1

Pick your kitchen

Tell it your diet and the kitchens you cook from: North Indian, Bengali, Gujarati and more. Their everyday dishes go straight onto your menu.

Step 2

Can't decide? Pick for me

Filter by meal or main ingredient, or tap Pick for me and let the app choose today's dish from your menu.

Step 3

Start cooking

See the ingredients and how long it takes, copy the shopping list, and open your saved recipe video, or search YouTube and Instagram for one.

Made for Indian homes

A cookbook that knows your rotation

Most families cook the same thirty-odd dishes on repeat. KhaanaKya keeps those front and centre, and the rest close at hand.

1,100+ home dishes

The Cookbook holds Indian home cooking from every region: dal-sabzi, breakfast staples, street food, mithai and chai-time snacks. Add any of them to your menu with one tap.

Veg, egg or non-veg

Choose what your household eats and the app only shows dishes that fit. Change it any time in Settings.

Your own dishes too

Cook something the Cookbook doesn't have? Add it with just a name, and paste the reel or video you learned it from.

Filters that find something

Breakfast, lunch/dinner or snacks. Paneer, dal, rice, meat. Narrow the menu down to what you're in the mood for, or what's in the fridge.
